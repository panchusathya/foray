**How to answer:** bullets, fragments, numbers, names. Do **not** write polished prose — I
compose, you supply raw material. A messy specific beats a clean generality every time.

**Priority key:** 🔴 blocks a specific answer · 🟡 makes an answer materially stronger ·
⚪ nice to have.

# 2 · Tushar — the drone company

**This is the biggest single unlock in your entire application.** Your YC form called it
*"prototyped a fixed-wing aircraft."* You actually **founded a company that won awards and
raised grant funding.** Those are not the same sentence, and every one of these five programs is
asking for the second one. a16z's whole form is built to find out whether you were a founder
before you called yourself one.

16. 🔴 **Name of the company.** Dates — start and end (or is it still alive?).
17. 🔴 **What did it actually make, and for whom?** One paragraph, plain language.
18. 🔴 **What did *you* personally do?** Did you write code, build airframes, do CAD, run the
    team, raise the money? Greylock asks for your specific role, and "founder" isn't an answer.
19. 🔴 **How many people?** Co-founders, team size at peak.
20. 🔴 **Which awards, which years, and what were they for?** ABC Innovation Prize — for what
    specifically? Blackstone Launchpad — finalist out of how many?
21. 🔴 **How much grant funding, and from whom?** Named sources and dollar amounts. "Grant
    funding" is a shrug; "$X from Y and Z" is a fact.
22. 🟡 **Did it fly?** Any flight footage, test data, photos? This is the highest-value link in
    your entire application if it exists.
23. 🟡 **Users, customers, pilots, revenue?** Anything beyond the prototype.
24. 🟡 **What was the hardest technical or operational problem you personally solved?**
25. 🔴 **How did it end?** If it failed or wound down: what happened, and what did you get wrong?
    **This is very likely your best answer to Z-Fellows' risk-and-failure question** and it's the
    kind of thing these programs reward far more than a clean win.
26. 🟡 **What did you learn there that you are using at Foray right now?**
27. ⚪ **Any press, a website, a repo, a demo day video?**

## What makes these answers land, so you can self-edit as you go

- **Denominators.** Not "grew the team" — "2 to 11 in six months." Not "grant funding" —
  "$40K from the Gene Haas Foundation."
- **A moment, not a summary.** "I realised the scarce resource at a conference isn't booth
  space" is why Tushar's Sapphire answer works. "I've always been entrepreneurial" is why most
  answers don't.
- **Unprompted action.** Every one of these programs is screening for things you did with no
  assignment, no grade and no boss. Weight those heavily.
- **A real failure.** Everyone dodges this and readers notice. The drone company, if it ended,
  is worth more than any clean win on this page.
- **One click of proof.** The SPC application had zero links. That alone is disqualifying at
  a16z, whose prompt is literally *"things you made exist."*
