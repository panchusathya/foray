# Which accelerators to network into — ranked

Dates are from your own tracker; the judgement is mine.

**The frame that changes the ranking.** You are two investors at an $8B fund with $0 revenue.
A cold application reads one way; a warm intro from a portfolio founder or a partner reads
completely differently. **For your profile specifically, the referral is worth more than the
essay** — you have unusual access and almost none of it is being used.

**The constraint nobody has priced in.** You go full-time at the end of the year. That rules
out anything starting before January, and it makes **January–February 2027 batches the natural
target for all of it.** Network now, apply October–November, start when you're actually free.

## How to approach them, given who you are

Being investors cuts both ways. Peer access is real, but a VC networking VCs reads as
transactional fast. **Lead with the insight, never the credential.**

The opener that works for you is not "we're investors at WestBridge building in recruiting." It
is the thing nobody else in the category can say:

> Every product in AI recruiting is sold against sourcing. We interviewed 15 recruiters and
> recorded eight — six of them said the middle of the funnel is where they actually lose
> candidates, and none of their vendors have built for it. So we built that first.

Then, if it's a fund with a portfolio: offer to work a live req for one of their companies for
free. **You have something almost no applicant has — a service you can render immediately.** A
partner who watches you deliver candidates to a portfolio company in a week doesn't need to read
your application.
