## If you only do four things this week

**1. 🔴 Collect a dollar.** Vela has $10K in writing; eight companies hold free work. **"$0 revenue"
is the weakest row in the entire pack and one signature deletes it.** Nothing else you can do in
seven days changes more.

**2. 🔴 Capture 25–50 standing briefs.** Your positioning is now *"we capture intent."* You have
captured intent from nobody. **This is the gap a sharp reader finds in ten seconds**, and it's
fixable in a week: text 100 engineers you already know, ask the five brief questions, log them.
Then A3 gains the only sentence that proves the thesis — *"N people have told us privately what
would move them, and none of it exists anywhere else."* That single line does more for the new
positioning than everything else in section A combined.

**3. 🔴 Get the browser agent live and submit real applications.** "Works in testing" and "we
submitted N applications autonomously on behalf of real candidates" are different companies.

**4. 🔴 Wire the rejection trigger and make it fire once.** Days of work on connectors you already
shipped. One real instance — *candidate connects Gmail, rejection lands, four matched roles within
minutes* — proves the acquisition loop the whole consumer thesis depends on, and it's the
cold-start problem you've been stuck on for two weeks.

**Cheap and non-negotiable:** the links (DroneCo deck, Walmart Loom, Sathya's repos), the exact
referral denominator, and one Slack message to three WestBridge partners about referrers.

and no revenue; another 20K lines changes nothing about how these forms read.
