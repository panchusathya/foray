Privacy Policy

Effective 27 August 2026 · Last updated 27 August 2026

Foray is a recruiting service for job candidates. We take your details, match you to open roles, prepare and submit applications on your behalf, and keep you posted on where each one stands. This policy explains what we collect while doing that, why, who else sees it, and what you can ask us to do about it.

This policy covers candidates — people using Foray to find work. It applies to our website, the candidate portal, our phone line, and our text conversations with you.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details — your name, phone number, and email address.
  • Your resume and the work history in it, plus profile details like your current title, skills, and education.
  • What you are looking for — target roles, seniority, locations, remote preference, industries, company stage and size, compensation expectations, timeline, and any companies you would rather we avoided.
  • Two work-authorization answers: whether you are legally authorized to work where you are applying, and whether you will need sponsorship.
  • Whatever else you choose to tell us in a call, a text, or the chat.

Calls

Our intake assistant is an AI. It says so at the start of every call, and it asks for your permission to record before it asks you anything else. If you say no, we do not record and we do not continue with intake — a person follows up instead. Permission is asked per call, not once and assumed forever. Where we record, we keep the recording and a written transcript.

Texts and chat

We keep the messages you exchange with us, including any files you send in a text thread, and the record of how and when you agreed to be texted.

Connected apps, only if you choose to connect one

You can connect Gmail, Outlook, or GitHub so we can find your resume instead of making you dig it out. If you do:

  • We request read-only access and nothing wider — Gmail gmail.readonly, Microsoft Mail.Read, GitHub read:user. We never request permission to send mail as you.
  • We run a fixed set of searches looking for resume attachments. We do not read your email for content, and we do not index your mailbox.
  • What we keep is the resume file itself and basic profile facts. The subject lines, bodies, and snippets of your email are not stored — there is no field in our system that holds them.
  • Your access tokens are encrypted at rest. We read your mailbox once when you connect, and again only if you explicitly ask us to re-check. There is no background sync.
  • You can disconnect at any time from the connect page. We delete our copy of the token and revoke it upstream where the provider offers a way to. Google and GitHub do; Microsoft does not, so we delete our copy and you can revoke it from your Microsoft account.

Automatically

Standard technical data when you use the portal: IP address, browser and device type, and log records of requests. We use this to keep the service running and secure.

What we never ask for

Salary history. We ask what you are targeting going forward. We never ask what you make now or made before, and if you mention it we do not record it or use it to steer anything.

Protected characteristics. We never ask about your age, race, religion, health, disability, family or marital status, or pregnancy. If you volunteer something in one of these categories, the assistant acknowledges it and moves on — there is no field for it to land in.

Immigration detail. Beyond the two authorization questions above, we do not ask about citizenship, visa type, green-card status, or country of origin.

How we use your information

  • To match you to open roles against the preferences you gave us.
  • To prepare applications — drafting a resume, cover letter, and answers for a specific role — and to submit them where you have asked us to.
  • To tell you where your applications stand and to answer your questions.
  • To introduce you to employers, where you have told us that is okay.
  • To keep a record of the permissions you gave us and when.
  • To operate, secure, debug, and improve the service.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not use your information to train third-party AI models, and we do not use it for advertising.

Text messaging

We text you only if you asked us to. There are two ways that happens: you tell us yes on a recorded call when we ask, verbatim, “Can I text you updates at this number?” — or you text our number first. Only a clear yes counts; we do not re-ask or rephrase to get a better answer.

Our first message identifies us and asks you to reply so we know we have the right number. Nothing else goes out until you do. After that, our messages are about your own job search: application status updates, roles that match what you told us, requests for a missing detail, and replies to what you send us.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories of data sharing described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; that information is not shared with any third party.

Reply STOP at any time and we stop texting you. Reply HELP for help. Message frequency varies with your own job search. Message and data rates may apply.

Our voice and text line is coming soon. We will publish the number here once it is live.

Who we share it with

Employers

The point of the service is getting your profile in front of companies that are hiring, and we ask you directly whether that is okay before we do it. When we apply on your behalf, the employer receives what any applicant would send: your resume, your cover letter, and your answers to their questions. Anything you tell us you would rather we did not repeat to a company, we do not.

Legal

We disclose information where the law requires it, to respond to lawful requests, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights and safety of people using the service. If we are ever involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may transfer as part of that, and this policy travels with it.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have not done so in the preceding twelve months. We do not buy, rent, or trade phone numbers, and every number we text belongs to someone who asked us to.

How long we keep it

We keep your profile, applications, and message history for as long as you are using Foray and for a reasonable period afterwards, so that a returning candidate does not start from nothing. We keep records of the permissions you gave us — including your text opt-in and the exact wording you saw or heard — for as long as we may need to show that consent was given, and for at least [RETENTION PERIOD] afterwards. You can ask us to delete your information at any time; see below.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop the texts — reply STOP, any time.
  • Disconnect an app — from the connect page, any time.
  • Stop us sharing with employers — tell us, and we stop.
  • See what we hold, correct it, or delete it — email privacy@goforay.io.

If you are a California resident, the CCPA and CPRA give you the right to know what we collect and why, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of sale or sharing (we do neither), and not to be discriminated against for exercising any of these. We will verify your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within the time the law allows. You may use an authorized agent.

Under California law, candidates are entitled to the pay scale for a role on request. Ask us and we will get it for you.

Automated decisions

Our assistants collect information and prepare applications. They do not decide whether you get hired — employers do. Where we rank roles, we are ranking them against the preferences you gave us. If you would rather a person looked at your file instead of the assistant, ask and we will arrange it.

Security

Access to candidate data is restricted to the people who need it. Connected-app tokens are encrypted at rest, traffic to our servers is encrypted in transit, and each organization's data is separated from every other's. No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise — but if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Minors

Foray is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If our assistant realizes it is talking to a minor, it stops the intake and hands off to a person. If you believe a minor has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this policy as the service changes. The effective date at the top always tells you which version you are reading, and we keep a record of the wording that was in place when you gave us each permission — so a change here never rewrites what you originally agreed to.
